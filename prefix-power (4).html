For teachers (tap to open)

Standards

ELAGSE3RF3a — Identify and know the meaning of the most common prefixes and derivational suffixes.

ELAGSE3L4b — Determine the meaning of a new word formed when a known affix is added to a known word.

Prefixes Included

un-, re-, pre-, dis-, mis- — these account for roughly 75% of all prefixed words students encounter in 3rd grade text.

Use it for

Word study warm-up, partner game, small-group rotation, fast-finisher activity, or independent vocabulary practice. Works on Chromebooks, iPads, and phones.

Differentiation

The word bank surfaces every available base word for the selected prefix; struggling readers can click instead of typing. Advanced students can hide the bank visually and challenge themselves to type from memory. The "Spotlight one" button highlights a single random word for kids who need a nudge.
